## 0. Opening

### 0.1 Shape

**World's job.** World assembles the Seed into the ecosystem: the entities Seed names, the value-chain relationships between them, and the assumption that justifies each relationship — why each party exists in the system, why they follow the party before them, why they enter, why they stay. World does not birth product treatment of those parties, write visibility grids, declare success measures, or name platform-craft paths.

Om Coda has a repeatable shape: kinds of insights it chases become products; each product has a way people are acquired into it and a business-model shape. Tower is one birth of that shape — an immigration-consultancy desk wrapped in always-on eligibility and engagement. Because Om Coda runs that product across firms as an agency, not as a self-serve tool each firm configures alone, the operations that run the product belong to an operator/house configuration-and-oversight layer. That layer is part of the world because of the agency shape.

| Layer | Ecosystem shape |
|---|---|
| **Acquisition** | **ALG** — one-tap Meta Approach yielding name + website + phone/email inside a click budget. Assisted / operator-led entry (**OLG**) is not deleted; it is a second real door into the same application. |
| **Activation** | **Forward-deploy + agent-earned hard inputs.** A no-login prepared campaign is served first; database authorization and escrow acceptance are earned after readiness is legible. |
| **Application** | Always-on loop over a private firm book: detection → engagement → enriched data → re-detection, toward a booked meeting. The operations that run the loop are house-configured; the firm inhabits the book and the meetings. |
| **Evolution** | **Named, lightly held.** Running-firm friction becomes a documented gap, then a written affordance or backend facet, then regenerates into the product. Not a client lifecycle phase. |

### 0.2 World sentence

> Tower is the always-on immigration-consultancy workspace that acquires a stranger consultant through a one-tap Meta Approach (name + website + channel within a click budget), activates by forward-deploying a no-login prepared campaign under their firm identity until they authorize database access and accept escrow, then runs the firm desk loop — keep the private contact book reachable, engage through opt-in → nudge → reactivation, refresh contact facts through touchpoints, re-evaluate service eligibility as facts and IRCC-shaped rules move, and campaign eligible people toward a booked meeting — without the consultant rechecking every file by hand, and without inventing public-before-contact detection of end-client eligibility. Om Coda runs this as an agency product: the operations that keep eligibility and engagement running are house-configured, not left for the firm to invent alone.

### 0.3 Facet doctrine

1. **Activation sets the target; acquisition fulfills the seed quota.** Acquisition does not get to decide what it collects — activation's finish line decides, and acquisition's only job is to deliver that quota within its budget.
2. **Finish lines are input sets, not a vague yes.** Acquisition ends when name + website + phone/email land. Activation ends when database authorization and escrow acceptance land and the campaign can run.
3. **Click budget lives in acquisition only.** Nothing hard may be pulled backwards into it.
4. **Forward-deploy lives in activation.** Serving a prepared workspace is not acquisition work, no matter how much it looks like marketing.
5. **Entry paths are product paths.** Approach gets the same Register discipline as the desk (decomposition, gap writing, affordance writing) — with the caveat that affordance writing on Approach is mostly persuasion texture, not desk inhabit.
6. **One Approach per acquired non-house party.** Leave which parties are acquired as a later treatment call. The Om Coda house is never an acquired stranger through Approach, and the engagement contact is never acquired through Approach at all.
7. **Application does not reshape to make the growth bet cheaper.** If the ALG bet needs fewer inputs, that is a growth problem, not a licence to shrink the desk.
8. **The desk is the same desk either way.** ALG-provisioned and assisted-provisioned firms land in one application, one session matrix.

### 0.4 Terms — each defined once, plain language

| Term | Plain meaning (used consistently below) |
|---|---|
| **Facet** | One of four layers a party can be served in: acquisition, activation, application, evolution. |
| **Acquisition** | Getting the cheap inputs needed to *provision* something for a stranger, autonomously, inside a click budget. Ends when those inputs land. |
| **Activation** | The systemized path from provisioned stranger to the last inputs required to *run*. Ends when the campaign can run. |
| **Application** | The desk performing once running — engage the book, refresh facts, re-evaluate eligibility, campaign toward a booked meeting. |
| **Evolution** | Product change driven by running-firm feedback through Register passes. |
| **Running** | State after the last activation input: the campaign performs without asking for more required activation inputs. |
| **ALG (agent-led growth)** | Cheap public inputs are given; the system extrapolates and prepares; an agent earns the hard inputs mid-activation. No sales call as the primary door, no long self-serve onboarding form. |
| **Assisted / operator-led entry** | A human at Om Coda collects the inputs (call, assisted onboarding, seeded provision). Still a real path beside ALG. |
| **Self-serve entry** | The party fills the forms themselves. Not the Tower bet. |
| **Approach** | The acquisition strip: feed context → ad → capture → continue your scroll. |
| **Seed inputs** | Firm/consultant name, website, and one channel (phone **or** email). |
| **Click budget** | The deliberate cap on stranger effort during acquisition. Forced at one click as a design constraint; relaxed only as a finding, because clicks are easier to add than to remove. |
| **Forward-deploy** | After seed inputs land: read public firm facts, instantiate Om Coda's own campaign templates, and stage a working campaign under the firm's identity — before the firm has given anything hard. |
| **Prepared workspace** | The no-login artifact that forward-deploy produces. Proves **readiness**, not value. |
| **Hard inputs** | The two things that cannot be inferred: authorization into the firm's database (or the equivalent that makes the campaign real), and acceptance of payment terms. |
| **Escrow** | Contingent commercial terms between the **firm and Om Coda** — cost is zero or held until release terms are met. Tower does not hold immigrant settlement funds. |
| **Contingent cost** | Om Coda's money posture: the buyer's cost is held until release terms are met. |
| **Public-detection products** | A different class of product where one cheap public fact (a legal business name, a parcel identifier) is already enough to run real detection. Tower is **not** one of these: real end-client eligibility needs the firm's own book. This is why Approach must not ask for a database inside the click budget. |
| **Audit** | A reachability gate the world requires over a set of contacts. One question only: can we reach this contact, and can we start a sequence on them? Validates contact data and may connect to external validation. Verdicts: reachable / partial / unreachable. Sequence-ready when passed. Not pathway scoring, not forecasting, not a sales pass/fail ceremony, not a minimum-contact-count gate. |
| **Agent sequence** | An ordered set of channel + copy steps. Launch and reactivation agents are composites of those steps. Composing them is configuration; sending them is automation. |
| **Campaign calendar** | The day-ordered layer of engagement content (opt-in / nudge / reactivation across days). Obeys Engine 2 precedence. Distinct from the attempt engine that runs one sequence at runtime. |
| **Attempt / channel-ownership engine** | Runtime law for a single engagement sequence: one channel owns the flow at a time; waits and attempts nest under that owner; motion is forward-only when criteria are met; a live inbound can override an in-flight attempt; a finishing event closes the sequence and can arm the next. |
| **Engagement record** | Chronology of engagement events that already fired. Authorship of channel order, attempt logic, and escalation lives upstream of the record — the record does not decide them. |
| **Automation workflow** | Trigger (pull scoped records) → conditions / rules evaluate → structured output → downstream actions or enrollment. The world is shaped so this graph holds across verticals; industry criteria packs swap underneath. |
| **Operator / house configuration layer** | Because Om Coda runs the product across many firms as an agency, the operations that run the product — evaluation rules, analysis, operational logic, data criteria — belong here, not to what the firm configures alone at the desk. This layer is part of the world because of that agency shape. |
| **Open-box evaluation** | Because the product decides and analyzes over data, the operations that produce answers must be inspectable and changeable by an operator without a code deploy — an open box, not a sealed one. Same necessity as the operator/house configuration layer. |
| **Engagement phases** | Opt-in (consent, first channel open) → re-engagement nudges (ongoing touchpoints that collect and refresh facts) → reactivation (re-engaging quiet contacts when rules or data warrant). |
| **Armed vs active** | Armed = template ready, nothing sending (firm-side ghost). Active = the sequence is executing. |
| **Silenced** | Opted out or otherwise excluded from automatic outreach; also the state of a contact who never opted in. |
| **Nudge** | A touchpoint that carries a data ask. Nudges are the data-collection mechanism, not spam. |
| **Signal vs motion** | Every detection — including a missing fact — is recorded as a **signal** when found. Whether anything is *sent* is a separate **motion** decision under fixed precedence. |
| **Engine 2 precedence** | Campaign-calendar law of how motion works in this world: reactivation first, always, if anything is reactivation-worthy. Nudge only when nothing is. One nudge consolidates every outstanding self-reportable need into a single form. When reactivation fires, other signals ride along as agenda on the consultant's pre-meeting brief — never as parallel outreach. **One client, one motion, ever.** Does not replace the attempt / channel-ownership engine inside a single sequence. |
| **Self-reportable** | A fact the client knows offhand (a score, a date, a status). Eligible for forms and pre-meeting capture. |
| **Document-dependent** | A fact requiring paperwork (credential assessments, labour-market paperwork, employer revenue). **Never** enters a form; becomes a tracked work item. |
| **Employer-directed** | The ask is aimed at the firm, not the client. Routed separately. |
| **Service eligibility** | What the firm can *sell* this person now, computed from firm-held plus engagement-collected facts against house-held or firm-configured rules. Pathway pass = **any** pathway assertion, not one favoured pathway. |
| **Reference tables** | Versioned immigration data (categories, trades, cutoffs, provincial identifiers) the house maintains as data inside the operator/house layer — updated without a code deploy. The law / public-reference side of eligibility change. |
| **Analysis** | The human-readable read-out of an evaluation: headlines, deltas, service candidates, nudge/reactivation hints. Machine output retained underneath. |
| **Live brief** | The consultant's pre-meeting view. Re-computed on write-back, not a snapshot. |
| **Loop-closer** | The behaviour at meeting-booked: outstanding self-reportable fields become a capture form pending the meeting; any write-back re-evaluates immediately. |

## 1. Ecosystem

Inventory and relationships only. Every party below appears because the ecosystem needs them. **No treatment rulings** — this section does not decide who gets a product path, who logs in, or what is built versus merely referenced.

### 1.1 Entity inventory

| Entity | Stated interest |
|---|---|
| **Firm (tenancy)** | Holds the private contact book; owns users and contacts as the tenancy boundary. |
| **Consultant / firm operator** | Recover booked consults and retainer starts from a dark list; stop spending time manually rechecking files as rules and facts move; avoid reputation damage from uninformed outreach. |
| **Engagement contact (Client)** | Timely advice when their situation or the rules changed and nobody re-engaged them; free of any payment to Om Coda. |
| **Om Coda house** | Runs Tower as an agency product across firms — not a self-serve tool each firm configures alone. Acquires and activates consultants into *running* tenancies under contingent cost; holds the operator/house configuration-and-oversight layer through which evaluation rules, analysis, operational logic, and data criteria stay configurable without a code deploy; maintains versioned public-reference criteria as part of that layer; operates the assisted door when ALG is not the path; holds escrow terms. |
| **Om Coda Approach / ads supply** | Reach consultants in a Meta feed; make one-tap output legible; capture name + website + channel inside the click budget; instrument *did not understand* versus *understood and did not tap*; treat continue-scroll as data. |
| **Om Coda reference-data upkeep** | House function inside the operator/house layer: keep versioned immigration categories, trades, cutoffs, and related tables current as data — so evaluations can move without a code deploy. This is the law / public-reference side of eligibility change. |
| **Om Coda Register / internal tooling** | House-side methodology tooling for how Om Coda writes and regenerates the product. Distinct from the firm's everyday desk, and distinct from the operator/house configuration layer that runs the product for firms. |
| **Agent presentation layer** | Present, ask, and route — on Approach, beside the prepared workspace, and toward desk actions. Never scrape, instantiate templates, fire sequences, evaluate rules, send OTP, or move money. |

**Not invented here.** There is no household entity (spouse/dependant facts live on the engagement contact record). Multi-consultant / roles / permissions inside a firm remain open — World does not mint additional firm-side parties for that. Incumbent CRMs, marketing tools, and Meta-as-platform are channels or substitutes named for contrast, not Tower ecosystem parties with a justifying stay-reason inside this chain. Product-shape drives a class of house/operator paths the firm party would never ask for; World names the necessity of that class, not the members of the class.

## 2. Shape necessities

World-shape that follows from how Om Coda and Tower exist — developed here so §0–§1 and this section read as one statement.

### 2.1 Om Coda shape and the operator/house layer

Om Coda has a repeatable shape: kinds of insights it chases become products; each product has a way people are acquired into it and a business-model shape. Tower is one birth of that shape — an immigration-consultancy desk wrapped in always-on eligibility and engagement.

Because Om Coda operates as an agency that runs the product on the firm's behalf — not a self-serve tool the firm configures alone — the operations that actually run the product (evaluation rules, analysis, operational logic, data criteria) belong to an **operator/house configuration-and-oversight layer**, not to what the firm configures alone at the desk. That layer is in the ecosystem because of the agency shape. Reference-data upkeep lives inside it; Register methodology tooling sits beside it as house writing practice, not as a substitute for it.

Across Om Coda products the preference is modular where it matters: the bets that run the product are operator-configurable without a code deploy, the way a CRM or data-vendor admin config is — not every cosmetic. This is world-shape. Open-box evaluation (§3.4) is the same necessity stated from the evaluation side.

### 2.2 Two sides of the detection coin

Eligibility changes are driven by two different sources. The split is why the product exists in the shape it does:

1. **Law / public-reference change** — scrapeable, storable, versionable criteria applied to client data (immigration matrix, constants, rules), held and versioned inside the operator/house layer.
2. **Life change on the contact** — facts only the person, or the firm's file after collection, can know.

Storing the firm's files and never reaching out serves only the first source. The second requires asking and telling — the engagement loop (nudges, forms) — which is also what makes the consent / PII posture necessary. Detection → engagement → enriched data → re-detection is one loop, not a read of a static dump.

### 2.3 Vertical modularity

The world is shaped for the same product to serve another vertical with a similar shape (always-on eligibility + engagement over a private book) by swapping industry criteria packs, not by forking. The graph shape — trigger → conditions / rules → actions that enroll sequences — is meant to hold across verticals; only the packs and cosmetics change.

## 3. Operational laws

Laws of how motion and evaluation work in this world. Terms in §0.4 and objects in §1.3 name the same laws; this section states them as necessities.

### 3.1 Reachability gate

The world requires a reachability gate (the audit batch) that answers one operational question: can we reach this contact and start a sequence? It validates contact data and connects to external validation where needed. Passed contacts are sequence-ready. It is not pathway scoring and not a sales pass/fail ceremony.

### 3.2 Engagement record is a record, not a decision brain

The engagement record holds events that already fired. Channel order, attempt logic, and escalation are authored upstream — not decided by the record. Examining the record is not authoring the motion.

### 3.3 Two sequencing layers, never conflated

Motion has two distinct layers — laws of how motion works in this world:

1. **Campaign calendar** — orders engagement content across days and obeys Engine 2 precedence: reactivation outranks nudge; one client, one motion.
2. **Attempt / channel-ownership engine** — runs a single sequence at runtime: one channel owns the flow at a time; waits and attempts nest under that owner; motion is forward-only when criteria are met; a live inbound can override an in-flight attempt; a finishing event closes the sequence and can arm the next.

Conflating calendar order with runtime channel ownership produces the wrong brain for either job.

### 3.4 Open-box evaluation

Because the product decides and analyzes over data, the operations that produce answers must be inspectable and changeable by an operator without a code deploy — an open box, not a sealed one. This is the same necessity as the operator/house configuration layer in §2.1: the agency shape puts those operations in the house layer, and open-box is how that layer must work.

## 4. Hard human gates

What genuinely requires a person. Founder-ratified inventory, not craft.

| Gate | Layer | Who acts | Why it cannot be automated away |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seed capture — name, website, channel | Acquisition | Consultant | A stranger must act; cheap by design |
| Firm and user provision (assisted door) | Application bootstrap | Operator | Tenancy is intentional, not self-minted |
| One-time-code verification | Application | Consultant | Proves control of the email |
| Database authorization | Activation | Consultant | A high-trust act over the firm's own book |
| Escrow acceptance | Activation | Consultant | Money and terms |
| Client consent / not silenced | Application | Engagement contact | Ethics and law |
| Meeting booking | Application | Engagement contact | Human commitment — and the core success event |
| Refusing illegal or unethical outreach | Application | Consultant | Firm responsibility, not platform policy |

**Not Tower gates** (they belong to other products' worlds): power of attorney before an officer, finder licensure, disbursement of public funds.
