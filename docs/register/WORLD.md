## 0. Opening

### 0.1 Shape

**World's job.** World assembles the Seed into the ecosystem: the entities Seed names, the value-chain relationships between them, and the assumption that justifies each relationship — why each party exists in the system, why they follow the party before them, why they enter, why they stay. World does not birth product treatment of those parties, write admission, declare outcomes, or name platform-craft surfaces.

| Layer | Ecosystem shape (from Seed) |
|---|---|
| **Acquisition** | **ALG** — one-tap Meta Approach yielding name + website + phone/email inside a click budget. Assisted / operator-led entry (**OLG**) is not deleted; it is a second real door into the same application. |
| **Activation** | **Forward-deploy + agent-earned hard inputs.** A no-login prepared campaign is served first; database authorization and escrow acceptance are earned after readiness is legible. |
| **Application** | Always-on firm desk loop over a private firm book. Once the firm is running, engagement is automation-led when armed — opt-in → nudge → reactivation toward a booked meeting. |
| **Evolution** | **Named, lightly held.** Running-firm friction becomes a documented gap, then a written affordance or backend facet, then regenerates into the product. Not a client lifecycle phase. |

### 0.3 World sentence

> Tower is the always-on immigration-consultancy workspace that acquires a stranger consultant through a one-tap Meta Approach (name + website + channel within a click budget), activates by forward-deploying a no-login prepared campaign under their firm identity until they authorize database access and accept escrow, then runs the firm desk loop — keep the private contact book reachable, engage through opt-in → nudge → reactivation, refresh Client Data through touchpoints, re-evaluate service eligibility as facts and IRCC-shaped rules move, and campaign eligible people toward a booked meeting — without the consultant rechecking every file by hand, and without inventing public-before-contact detection of end-client eligibility.

### 0.5 Facet doctrine

1. **Activation sets the target; acquisition fulfills the seed quota.** Acquisition does not get to decide what it collects — activation's finish line decides, and acquisition's only job is to deliver that quota within its budget.
2. **Finish lines are input sets, not a vague yes.** Acquisition ends when name + website + phone/email land. Activation ends when database authorization and escrow acceptance land and the campaign can run.
3. **Click budget lives in acquisition only.** Nothing hard may be pulled backwards into it.
4. **Forward-deploy lives in activation.** Serving a prepared workspace is not acquisition work, no matter how much it looks like marketing.
5. **Entry surfaces are product surfaces.** Approach gets the same Register discipline as the desk (decomposition, admission, gap writing, affordance writing) — with the caveat that affordance writing on Approach is mostly persuasion texture, not desk inhabit.
6. **One Approach per acquired non-operator persona.** Leave which parties are acquired as a later treatment call. Seed doctrine also holds that the Om Coda house is never an acquired stranger through Approach, and that the engagement contact is never acquired through Approach at all.
7. **Application does not reshape to make the growth bet cheaper.** If the ALG bet needs fewer inputs, that is a growth problem, not a licence to shrink the desk.
8. **The desk is the same desk either way.** ALG-provisioned and assisted-provisioned firms land in one application, one session matrix.

### 0.6 Terms — each defined once, plain language

| Term | Plain meaning (used consistently below) |
|---|---|
| **Facet** | One of four layers a persona can be served in: acquisition, activation, application, evolution. |
| **Acquisition** | Getting the cheap inputs needed to *provision* something for a stranger, autonomously, inside a click budget. Ends when those inputs land. |
| **Activation** | The systemized path from provisioned stranger to the last inputs required to *run*. Ends when the campaign can run. |
| **Application** | The desk performing the core outcome once running. |
| **Evolution** | Product change driven by running-firm feedback through Register passes. |
| **Running** | State after the last activation input: the campaign performs without asking for more required activation inputs. |
| **ALG (agent-led growth)** | Cheap public inputs are given; the system extrapolates and prepares; an agent earns the hard inputs mid-activation. No sales call as the primary door, no long self-serve onboarding form. |
| **Assisted / operator-led entry** | A human at Om Coda collects the inputs (call, assisted onboarding, seeded provision). Still a real path in V1. |
| **Self-serve entry** | The persona fills the forms themselves. Not the Tower V1 bet. |
| **Approach** | The acquisition strip of surfaces: feed context → ad → capture → continue your scroll. |
| **Seed inputs** | Firm/consultant name, website, and one channel (phone **or** email). |
| **Click budget** | The deliberate cap on stranger effort during acquisition. Forced at one click as a design constraint; relaxed only as a finding, because clicks are easier to add than to remove. |
| **Forward-deploy** | After seed inputs land: read public firm facts, instantiate Om Coda's own campaign templates, and stage a working campaign under the firm's identity — before the firm has given anything hard. |
| **Prepared workspace** | The no-login artifact that forward-deploy produces. Proves **readiness**, not value. |
| **Hard inputs** | The two things that cannot be inferred: authorization into the firm's database (or the equivalent that makes the campaign real), and acceptance of payment terms. |
| **Escrow** | Contingent commercial terms between the **firm and Om Coda** — cost is zero or held until outcome terms are met. Tower does not hold immigrant settlement funds. |
| **Contingent cost** | Om Coda's money posture: the buyer's cost is held until outcome terms are met. |
| **Public-detection products** | A different class of product where one cheap public fact (a legal business name, a parcel identifier) is already enough to run real detection. Tower is **not** one of these: real end-client eligibility needs the firm's own book. This is why Approach must not ask for a database inside the click budget. |
| **Audit** | A reachability gate over a set of contacts. One question only: can we reach this contact, and can we start a sequence on them? Verdicts: reachable / partial / unreachable. Not scoring, not forecasting, not a pass/fail sales ceremony, not a minimum-contact-count gate. |
| **Agent sequence** | An ordered set of channel + copy steps. Launch and reactivation agents are composites of those steps. Composing them is configuration; sending them is automation. |
| **Automation workflow** | Trigger (pull scoped records) → conditions / rules evaluate → structured output → downstream actions or enrollment. |
| **Engagement phases** | Opt-in (consent, first channel open) → re-engagement nudges (ongoing touchpoints that collect and refresh facts) → reactivation (re-engaging quiet contacts when rules or data warrant). |
| **Armed vs active** | Armed = template ready, nothing sending (firm-side ghost). Active = the sequence is executing. |
| **Silenced** | Opted out or otherwise excluded from automatic outreach; also the state of a contact who never opted in. |
| **Nudge** | A touchpoint that carries a data ask. Nudges are the data-collection mechanism, not spam. |
| **Signal vs motion** | Every detection — including a missing fact — is recorded as a **signal** when found. Whether anything is *sent* is a separate **motion** decision under fixed precedence. |
| **Engine 2 precedence** | Reactivation first, always, if anything is reactivation-worthy. Nudge only when nothing is. One nudge consolidates every outstanding self-reportable need into a single form. When reactivation fires, other signals ride along as agenda on the consultant's pre-meeting brief — never as parallel outreach. **One client, one motion, ever.** |
| **Self-reportable** | A fact the client knows offhand (a score, a date, a status). Eligible for forms and pre-meeting capture. |
| **Document-dependent** | A fact requiring paperwork (credential assessments, labour-market paperwork, employer revenue). **Never** enters a form; becomes a tracked work item. |
| **Employer-directed** | The ask is aimed at the firm, not the client. Routed separately. |
| **Service eligibility** | What the firm can *sell* this person now, computed from firm-held plus engagement-collected facts against shipped or firm-configured rules. Pathway pass = **any** pathway assertion, not one favoured pathway. |
| **Reference tables** | Versioned immigration data (categories, trades, cutoffs, provincial identifiers) that Tower maintains as data — updated without a code deploy. |
| **Analysis** | The human-readable read-out of an evaluation: headlines, deltas, service candidates, nudge/reactivation hints. Machine output retained underneath for power users. |
| **Live brief** | The consultant's pre-meeting view. Re-computed on write-back, not a snapshot. |
| **Loop-closer** | The behaviour at meeting-booked: outstanding self-reportable fields become a capture form pending the meeting; any write-back re-evaluates immediately. |

## 5. Never invent — plain language

Each of these is a world bug. They come straight from Seed's hard stops.

1. **Detecting an end-client's immigration eligibility from public sources before anyone has contacted them, as core function.** That is a different class of product — one where a single cheap public fact is already enough to run detection. Tower's detection needs the firm's own book. Other Om Coda bets may work that way; Tower cannot.
2. **An agent seat, or an agent column in any admission matrix.** The agent is a presentation feature and has no capabilities of its own.
3. **A full client-side console mirroring the firm's workspace.** The end-client seat is touchpoint-only until the open question about client desk depth closes.
4. **The Om Coda house operator as a firm login persona.** Operator exists for lattice closure on acquisition and activation. It is not a Board seat, not a desk user, and not a third persona inside a firm's product.
5. **Prepaid credits, or a sales call, offered as equal alternatives at the activation payment moment.** Credits are prepayment and break the contingent promise. A sales call as a peer door turns agent-led growth into operator-led growth. Both may exist elsewhere; neither is a peer there.
6. **Database authorization or payment inside acquisition or the click budget.** Both are activation, always.
7. **Treating forward-deploy as acquisition.** Preparing a workspace looks like marketing and is activation work.
8. **Skipping client consent because the growth path was agentic.** A firm authorizing its own database is not its clients consenting to be messaged.
9. **Cold email as the V1 acquisition channel on the strength of an idea.** Email earns entry on evidence from ads, not on argument.
10. **Contingent terms as the only money model forever.** Escrow is the *first* money door for agent-led growth. Subscription or usage packaging is permitted later as an open question — it simply cannot stand beside escrow at the activation moment.
11. **Power of attorney before an officer, finder licensure, or disbursement of public funds as Tower gates.** Those belong to other products' worlds.
12. **Treating an external sales assessment document as the in-app reachability product.** Collateral may sell trust; the in-app surface stays operational.
13. **Claiming live backend persistence, or a production acquisition strip, as shipped.** Nothing in acquisition or activation is built.
14. **Reshaping the application desk so activation needs fewer inputs.** That move was explicitly not made.
15. **Sending outreach because something was detected.** Detection is a signal; sending is a separate decision under fixed precedence.
16. **Two motions on one client at once, or one message per missing field.** One client, one motion, ever; one consolidated form.
17. **Presenting the reachability gate as a score, a forecast, or a minimum-contact threshold.** One question only.
18. **Describing automation or wiring work as something the agent did.** See §8.

## Underlying assumptions pass (BA)

### A. Ecosystem / product (general) — unspoken assumptions

**1. There are enough immigration consultancies in the target geography to make a paid acquisition channel worth building at all.** — *Why worth considering:* A few hundred targetable firms behaves like a sales list, not an ad market, and would flip the whole growth bet back to operator-led.

**2. Contact details in a dormant firm book survive the years of dormancy well enough that reachability is meaningfully above zero.** — *Why worth considering:* The docs treat a dirty list as a teachable byproduct; if decay is the norm rather than the exception, reachability is the product's real ceiling.

**3. Immigration rules move often enough to keep producing re-evaluation events, but not so fast that shipped rule packs are wrong most of the time.** — *Why worth considering:* Retention rests on rule movement generating fresh signals; too little movement and the loop goes quiet after the first sweep, too much and the packs are a liability.

**4. A contact's eligibility can change materially without the firm finding out through ordinary means.** — *Why worth considering:* If people simply call their consultant when their situation changes, always-on detection is solving a problem the phone already solves.

**5. Dormant contacts have not already retained another consultant.** — *Why worth considering:* The addressable value of a book assumes the relationship is re-openable rather than closed by a competitor.

**6. Firms have spare capacity to take the extra meetings — the bottleneck is lead flow, not consultant hours.** — *Why worth considering:* If the desk is already full, more booked meetings are a cost, not a win, and the core success metric stops being desirable.

**7. A booked meeting converts to paid work often enough that contingent terms can be priced against it.** — *Why worth considering:* Escrow tied to an outcome requires the outcome to carry money; a cheap meeting makes the release term unfundable.

**8. Asserting what a firm can sell someone is commercial triage, not the practice of immigration consulting.** — *Why worth considering:* If regulators read a service-eligibility assertion as advice, the rule packs become a licensing question rather than a data question.

**9. Professional regulators do not restrict automated or bulk solicitation by licensed consultants.** — *Why worth considering:* Sequences firing under a firm's name are a marketing act by a regulated professional; a conduct rule against it would gate the whole loop.

**10. Eligibility is computable from a bounded, mostly deterministic fact set.** — *Why worth considering:* The signal/motion law assumes a rule engine can reach a defensible verdict; if real files turn on discretion and edge judgement, Analysis becomes a suggestion box.

**11. Self-reported facts are accurate enough to base a service pitch on.** — *Why worth considering:* Scores, dates and work years drive pathway verdicts; systematic optimism in self-report produces confident wrong eligibility at scale.

**12. A wrong eligibility read is recoverable because the consultant catches it in the meeting.** — *Why worth considering:* This is the safety valve that makes shipping imperfect rules acceptable, and nothing in the flow enforces that the check actually happens.

**13. Email and text at book-scale will actually be delivered.** — *Why worth considering:* Domain reputation, carrier filtering and re-engaging years-cold addresses are exactly the conditions that trigger spam handling; the loop assumes messages land.

**14. Either the firm's own sending identity is technically available, or a substitute can carry the firm-branded promise.** — *Why worth considering:* "Firm → client value-add" depends on the message looking like it came from the firm; the sending-identity mechanics are never named.

### C. Engagement contact (Client) — unspoken assumptions

**1. They remember the firm and recognize its name.** — *Why worth considering:* The entire consent and trust posture rests on an existing relationship being felt, not merely recorded.

**2. They gave real contact details originally rather than a throwaway.** — *Why worth considering:* Reachability is judged on format validity; intent behind the original detail is invisible to the gate.

**3. Their phone number still belongs to them.** — *Why worth considering:* Recycled numbers mean immigration-related messages reaching a stranger, which is both a privacy event and a false reachable verdict.

**4. They are still in the immigration journey and have not already landed, given up, or left.** — *Why worth considering:* A settled or departed contact is unengageable, and the addressable share of a book depends on this proportion.

**5. If their own file is closed, they still hold value through family or referral.** — *Why worth considering:* Otherwise a successful firm's book is the least valuable one, which inverts who the best customer is.

**6. They will report facts accurately rather than favourably.** — *Why worth considering:* Optimistic language scores and work histories produce confident false eligibility, and the form has no verification layer.

**7. They understand the questions being asked.** — *Why worth considering:* Self-reportable assumes the person knows and can state the fact; program vocabulary defeats that more often than the classification implies.

**8. They read the language the touchpoint is written in well enough to answer.** — *Why worth considering:* Language ability is literally one of the facts being assessed, so low proficiency is a population feature, not an edge case.

**9. They will trust an automated firm-branded message in a market saturated with immigration fraud.** — *Why worth considering:* This population is heavily targeted by scams, so the trust bar for an unexpected message about their status is unusually high.

**10. They will not be unsettled that the firm still holds their old details and knows their situation.** — *Why worth considering:* The same message reads as attentive service or as surveillance depending on how the relationship ended.

**11. They have not retained another consultant in the interim.** — *Why worth considering:* Being re-approached by an old firm mid-engagement elsewhere is an awkward event with no handling in the model.

**12. They accept a system rather than their consultant re-opening the conversation.** — *Why worth considering:* The relationship was originally human; the resumption is not, and that shift is never acknowledged.

**13. They will complete a consolidated form rather than abandon a long one.** — *Why worth considering:* One-form consolidation is chosen to avoid nudge spam, and it trades that risk for abandonment risk that goes unmeasured.

**14. They will answer without any incentive.** — *Why worth considering:* The value exchange is future advice; the effort is now, and free-to-the-contact is treated as sufficient motivation.

**15. They can answer the self-reportable questions without digging out paperwork.** — *Why worth considering:* The forms-versus-tracked-work split assumes a clean line between remembered and documented facts, and dates and scores often sit on the wrong side of it.

**16. They act as an individual decision-maker.** — *Why worth considering:* Immigration decisions are usually household decisions, and a single contact record cannot carry a joint deliberation.

**17. They can and will report a spouse's or dependant's facts on request.** — *Why worth considering:* Several real pathway outcomes need those facts, and the docs explicitly refuse a household entity.

**18. They will not share the form link with others.** — *Why worth considering:* An unauthenticated capture link circulating in a community group contaminates the record and the eligibility read.

**19. Their going quiet reliably means disinterest.** — *Why worth considering:* Reactivation criteria treat silence as a state; a spam folder, a full inbox or a changed number produce the same silence with the opposite meaning.

**20. They can find and use the way to stop the messages.** — *Why worth considering:* Silenced is a state the model relies on, and a person who cannot opt out escalates instead.

**21. They will not escalate to a regulator or a platform when annoyed.** — *Why worth considering:* One complaint against a regulated professional costs the firm far more than the campaign earned.

**22. A meeting is a low enough commitment that they will take it.** — *Why worth considering:* The core success event assumes talking is free and easy; a paid or formal consultation is a very different conversion.

**23. Booked implies attended often enough for the metric to mean something.** — *Why worth considering:* The product measures booking, the firm values attendance, and no-show rates in cold-reactivated audiences are typically high.

**24. They can afford immigration services if eligible.** — *Why worth considering:* Eligibility and purchasing ability are separate; the loop optimizes only the first.

**25. Their circumstances change on a rhythm the nudge cadence can track.** — *Why worth considering:* Cadence is a design choice while life events are not, and a mismatch means either stale data or over-messaging.

**26. Facts reported at capture are still true at the meeting.** — *Why worth considering:* The brief is live with respect to write-back, not with respect to the world; nothing re-asks.

**27. They will stay tolerant of periodic messages over years.** — *Why worth considering:* Fatigue accumulates across the relationship even when each individual touch is well-behaved.

**28. They are not receiving similar outreach from several other firms.** — *Why worth considering:* If re-engagement becomes common practice, the differentiator is inbox competition rather than rule depth.

**29. The one channel the firm holds is the one they actually use.** — *Why worth considering:* Reachability is judged per channel, and an old work email passes validation while reaching nobody.

**30. They will click a link from a message on a phone.** — *Why worth considering:* Forms and capture pages assume a device, a browser and a willingness to leave the message.

**31. Being told what they might qualify for reads as helpful rather than as false hope or pressure.** — *Why worth considering:* The framing is what makes the value-add legitimate; misread, it becomes exactly the uninformed outreach the firm fears.

**32. They will not ask who the third party is.** — *Why worth considering:* Firm-branded delivery only holds if the vendor stays invisible, and any disclosure requirement breaks that.

**33. Their original consent, or the relationship itself, covers this new kind of contact.** — *Why worth considering:* Consent for a past file is narrower than consent for ongoing marketing, and the difference lands on the firm.

**34. A silenced contact staying silenced forever is acceptable.** — *Why worth considering:* Re-consent is unspecified, so the practical outcome is permanent loss of anyone who ever paused the relationship.

**35. Urgency in their own timeline — an expiry, a draw, a status change — makes them responsive.** — *Why worth considering:* This is the implicit hook that makes nudges welcome; without it every touchpoint is unprompted marketing.

**36. They do not need identity verification before their facts are trusted.** — *Why worth considering:* Service eligibility is asserted from unverified self-report attached to an unauthenticated touchpoint.

**37. They will not treat the firm's re-engagement as a claim of ongoing representation.** — *Why worth considering:* A message about eligibility from a former consultant can reasonably be read as continued professional responsibility.

**38. Their answers can be trusted as coming from them.** — *Why worth considering:* Family members, agents and community helpers frequently answer on someone's behalf in this domain.

**39. They will not expect a reply on their own terms.** — *Why worth considering:* Automated outreach invites inbound questions, and touchpoint-only gives them nowhere to land.

**40. Nothing in their situation makes contact itself risky for them.** — *Why worth considering:* For some statuses, an unsolicited message about immigration standing arriving on a shared device is a real-world harm the model never weighs.
