### Leaf 1.1 — see armed / active under my name

**Q:** How do I see what is armed or active under my name?

**Clarity:** Starting from Board, scan Client rows and Phase signals for what is in motion. On a Client, open Engagement record to examine what already fired — chronology only, not authorship.

**Criteria — when:** Anytime after running.  
**Conditions:** Engagement record is a record, not a decision brain; packs remain operator-authored.

| UI | Kind | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Board | Module | **existing** |
| Client row / Phase signal | Block | **existing** |
| Engagement record (Activity) | Block | **existing** |

**Requirements:** Sequence health detail may also appear on operator Firm health — consultant sees firm-scoped inhabit only.

### Leaf 1.2 — halt outreach I won't stand behind

**Q:** How do I halt any outreach I won't stand behind?

**Clarity:** On Board (or on Client), open Halt outreach and stop further automatic motion for that contact or for the firm book I won't stand behind. Halt is refusal under my license — not editing the house template.

**Criteria — when:** When the consultant refuses illegal or unethical outreach (hard human gate).  
**Conditions:** Halt is available without opening Configuration libraries; bound packs respect silence/halt.

| UI | Kind | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Board | Module | **existing** |
| Halt outreach | Modal | **new** |
| Client | Modal / Module pane | **existing** (client workspace) |

**Requirements:** Operator Firm operations bind and engagement runners must honor halt. Operator Support may see halt events via Audit trail.
