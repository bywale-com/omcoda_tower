## What the handler does

`handleContactSeatFacts` resolves the link, reads a body of `{ answers: [{ fieldKey, value }] }`, and for each non-empty key inserts `fact_writebacks`, deletes the matching `missing_fact_signals` row, calls `runContactEligibilityCascade`, dispatches `fact_writebacks`, and calls `onCompletionWrite`. The cascade payload’s source is the literal `contact_workspace`. The body has no consent fields and no source. `resolveFormStamp` puts `campaign_objective_id` on the row from the newest enrollment that has a campaign. `onCompletionWrite` loads that enrollment, and when the objective’s `completion_table` is `fact_writebacks` and a fact row carries that objective id, it advances the cursor. The function does not skip `pursuit_state` `exhausted`. `obj.optin.day` completes on `fact_writebacks` and sits on `camp.optin.day`. The seed contact `cr-harbor-4` at `firm-harbor` is the Ignore row, on `enr-harbor-4` for that campaign, with self-reportable missing facts. A post of those keys is the collection claim 28 said Ignore does not perform, and it is a way for that cursor to move after the click that claim 28 left still.
