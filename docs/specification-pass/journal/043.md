## The constant is one boolean doing two jobs

The cascade sets `anyPass` to `true` before it inserts `eligibility_results`. The column `any_pathway_pass` receives that constant. `result` receives `{ trigger, ...resultPayload }` and nothing else: fact scalars on a fact firing, `{ refId }` on a reference firing. The next line is `const motion = anyPass ? "reactivation" : "nudge"`. The motion row stores that string and `reason.precedence` `mp-1` for either string. The signal row stores `{ resultId, anyPass }`. After the loop, one dispatch sends `anyPass: true` and `motion: "reactivation"` as literals, not the variables just computed. `dispatchEventToWorkflows` copies the payload and sets `anyPass: true` again after the spread, so a false on the cascade’s object would not reach the walk. The trigger label `event:eligibility.changed` also contains the substring `eligibility`, and `walkValidatedWorkflow` treats that as `eligibilityPass`.

Claim 52 had already refused to branch on that payload motion, because one firing can hold two contacts and the branch reads `motion_decisions.motion`. Claim 54 enrolls re-engagement when that column is `nudge`. Both claims left the constant alone so they would not invent the predicate that fills the column. The false side therefore still had nobody, which is why this session started here.

Seed §5.7 is the law for the column. Pathway pass means any pathway assertion, CEC or FSW or FST or PNP-*, not one favoured pathway. The seed row `epath-any` stores that law as `set_value` `any_pathway_pass`. `epath-cec` and `epath-fsw` are enabled. `epath-pnp` is stored `enabled` false. There is no FST row, even though the aggregate label names FST. `when_clause` and `condition_clause` are null on all four. `SYSTEMS-AUTOMATIONS-ASCRIBE.md` A2 says the walker peeks `rule_eligibility_pathway` with `enabled=true` limited to one row, and that the peek is a pass flag rather than the GUI matrix. That select is not in `server/`. `listModularRules` can list the rows. The cascade does not call it. Reading `epath-any`’s enabled bit and calling it the contact’s pass would mark every contact true. That is the constant under another name.

The version 1 specimen already pairs the pieces the constant collapses. Okonkwo’s `result.pathways` has CEC and FSW passing and PNP failing, `any_pathway_pass` true, signal `anyPass` true, motion `reactivation`. Alvarez’s pathways fail, the boolean is false, the signal’s `anyPass` is false, and the motion is `nudge`. Both motion rows store precedence `mp-1`. Claim 52 used that shared key as a reason not to compare precedence. The specimen’s PNP entry on Okonkwo is teaching data: the seed row is disabled, so a specified write does not copy a disabled family onto the array.

## Three outputs, and only one of them is the column

The handoff said to read the eligibility rule output before inventing a second flag. There are two outputs, and neither is the cascade.

The walker rule node, `executeRuleWrites`, inserts `missing_fact_signals` for self-reportable keys that have no fact. Its `lastOutput.pass` is true when that insert wrote nothing. `nudge` and `reactivation` on the same object default true from `nudgeSignalOn` and `reactivationSignalOn`. If `ctx.eligibilityPass` is still unset, the walk sets it from that empty missing-fact list. The node does not insert `eligibility_results`. The cascade does not call the node. A contact with a full fact list would be “pass” here even when no pathway asserts, and a missing field would be “fail” even when one does. That pass is the missing-fact question. It is not the column.

The client pack, `evaluateRulePack`, is the other output. `buildPathwayOutcome` sets pathway `pass` from `asserted.length > 0`. The narrative says overall pathway pass means any asserting pathway. That OR is the shape of Seed §5.7. The function also asserts FST and three PNP result ids, which the enabled seed rows do not include. `buildSummary` then computes a different boolean. `summary.pass` is true only when every enabled critical family passes, and the critical families are pathway and draw. A CEC assertion under the cutoff would fail that summary and still be an any-pathway pass. `summary.reactivation.warranted` is true only for work-permit urgency, `R-OPS-05`. `summary.nudge.needed` is that urgency or any gap result. Using the reactivation signal as the motion would be a second flag. The expression already reads `anyPass`, and the column that stores `anyPass` is `any_pathway_pass`. The pack’s overall pass and its warrant flag stay off the column.

`mp-1` and `mp-2` are the precedence rows for the expression, not a second compare. `mp-1` sets `reactivation` when anything is reactivation-worthy. `mp-2` sets `nudge` when nothing is. The live insert writes `mp-1` even for a nudge, which is why claim 52 would not read the key. Once the expression can actually produce `nudge`, the reason stores `mp-2`, because that row’s `set_value` is `nudge`. The branch still reads `motion`.

The firing payload stays one object. Changing it to the last contact’s motion, or stopping the dispatcher from forcing `anyPass: true`, would put two contacts on one branch. Claim 52 already refused that. The per-contact boolean lives on the result row, the signal payload, and the motion row.

That is claim 60. `result` keeps the trigger object and gains `pathways`: one entry per enabled family, `id` taken from `set_value` without the `pathway_` prefix, `label` from the row, `pass` the family’s assertion. The column is the OR. Empty pathways are false. Disabled PNP is not an entry. FST is not an entry until a row exists. The motion expression reads the OR. Precedence follows the motion. No second flag.

## The family’s pass is the silence, and the three encoders do not agree

What sets `pass` on one family is the sentence Seed §5.8 and Story §11 left silent, and that `SYSTEMS-REQUIREMENTS-FILLED.md` said not to invent. The SQL clauses do not contain it. Two other encoders do, and they choose different people.

The pack’s formula is a current assertion. CEC needs Canadian skilled hours at `cec.min_hours` (1560 in `pack.immigration.criteria`), CLB at `cec.min_clb_all` (7), and a TEER outside `teer.ineligible` (`4,5`). FSW needs foreign work years at `fsw.min_foreign_years` (1), CLB at `fsw.min_clb_all` (7), and the same TEER test. `RULE_LEFT_FIELD_MAP` names the collectible keys. The walker already knows how to read a left side from `fact_writebacks` and a right side from `constants_packs`. The pathway rows do not hold those conditions, and the cascade does not call the pack. Under this encoder a contact who currently clears CEC is `reactivation` on every later cascade, and a contact with none of the facts is `nudge`.

elig-04 in `docs/sme/pass2/01-immigration-pathway-eligibility.md` is a material change. Newly met program-minimum, a cutoff newly crossed, a nomination or offer or certificate newly held, or a time-critical ops loss is reactivation-worthy. Pure missing self-reportables stay nudge. A stable CEC pass would not re-enter reactivation. An ops loss could be reactivation while every family `pass` is false. That is not the OR. Adopting it as the motion, while the column stays the OR, would be the second flag this session was told not to invent. Adopting it as the family’s `pass` would make the OR mean “changed,” which is not what Seed §5.7 says an assertion is.

The empty clauses are a third filling if someone treats them as the predicate. No condition means no assertion, so every contact is `nudge`, claim 52 enrolls nobody, and claim 54 enrolls the book. That is a choice the seed did not write. The opposite reading, the aggregate row’s enabled bit, is the live constant, and claim 60 refuses it.

Storing `pass: false` on every family until the founder answers would smuggle the third filling in as a default. The live constant stays the gap. It is not a specified false. The shape is specified so the next session does not re-derive the OR, the enabled set, or which outputs are not the column.

The founder question is which encoder sets the family’s `pass`, because that choice is who claim 52 enrolls and who claim 54 enrolls. Seed §5.7 settles the OR. It does not settle the assertion. The product direction does not pick among the pack, the SME note, and the empty clauses.

The thread this opened and did not walk is the other half of Seed §5.8: every detection, including missing-data, is recorded as a signal. The rule node writes `missing_fact_signals`. This firing writes one `signals` row, kind `eligibility_determination`. Those are different tables. Whether the missing-fact row is also a `signals` insert is the next read. It is not the family’s pass.
