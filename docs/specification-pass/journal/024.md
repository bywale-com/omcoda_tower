## What the handler does

`handleFactWriteback` is `POST /machine/fact-writebacks`. The firm comes from `requireSessionFirm`. The body carries `contactId`, `fieldKey`, `value`, and optional `source`, `missingFieldKeys`, and `formRef`. An empty contact or field is the existing 400. Past that check the handler inserts `fact_writebacks`, deletes the answered `missing_fact_signals` row, may insert more missing-fact rows, runs `runContactEligibilityCascade`, dispatches `fact_writebacks`, and calls `onCompletionWrite`. The cascade source is the body string, or `contact` when the body omits it. The insert does not store `source`. The table has no source column. `onCompletionWrite` can advance `obj.optin.day`, whose name is “They opt in” and whose completion table is `fact_writebacks`. The seed contact `cr-harbor-4` at `firm-harbor` is the Ignore row, on `enr-harbor-4` for `camp.optin.day`, with missing self-reportable facts. A post of those keys is the collection claim 29 withheld on the seat, through this other function, and it is a way for that cursor to move.

That finding mattered because the seat gate and this post are different functions. Closing the seat does not close this insert.
