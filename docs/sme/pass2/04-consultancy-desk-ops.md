## Focus strip A — Minimum inhabit signals to stand behind automated firm→client motion

### desk-01

**Question:** What minimum inhabit signals must a licensee see before they can responsibly authorize always-on firm→client outreach under their registration, without configuring sequences themselves?

**References:**
- Code of Professional Conduct for College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants Licensees, SOR/2022-128, ss. 4, 38 (integrity; supervise and assume professional responsibility for work done by assistants) — https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-128/FullText.html
- College Interpretation Guide to the Code (supervision / agents acting under authority) — https://college-ic.ca/protecting-the-public/code-of-professional-conduct
- Outcomes A · Core (hand over · authorize · receive); How `consultant-core` Leaf 2a.2 License acknowledgement
- World hard gate: Refusing illegal or unethical outreach — Consultant

**Thesis gap:** License acknowledgement at Accept terms is a one-shot door; practice still requires ongoing legibility of *what motion is armed or active under the firm name*. Without a defensible minimum inhabit set, “hands-free” becomes unsupervised automation the RCIC cannot stand behind.

**Solution:** Require firm-scoped inhabit of (1) identity under which sends run, (2) armed vs active posture for the bound book, (3) per-contact phase / sequenced-or-silenced state, and (4) a chronology of what already fired — as the standing accountability surface for license-backed motion — so that authorization is not a blind handoff and the consultant can refuse without becoming the pack author.

**Handoff:** pm (primary inhabit outcomes / never-sees); both where send-identity and armed/active must be durable state for runners

## Focus strip B — Live brief contents vs snapshot risk

### desk-07

**Question:** What must a live pre-meeting brief contain for an immigration consult so the consultant can take the meeting without re-interviewing from scratch — and without treating service-eligibility narrative as retained advice?

**References:**
- World glossary: Live brief = re-computed on write-back, not a snapshot; Loop-closer at meeting_booked
- How `contact-book` Leaf 1.2; How `consultant-core` Leaf 2b.2
- SOR/2022-128 ss. 6, 22–24 (honesty/candour; quality of service / keep client informed; consultation agreement before initial consultation)
- Essential Competencies §2.1 (preliminary assessment of client profiles); §8.2 (prepare for meeting; summarize outcomes)
- VisaFlo / practice norm: intake before consult so the first 30 minutes are not spent on basics — https://visaflo.ca/blog/building-your-immigration-practice-a-simple-tech-stack-guide-for-rcics-120237

**Thesis gap:** Seed locks “live, not snapshot” and Assump. 10, but desk contents are unspecified. Snapshot briefs create wrong advice risk; over-claiming Analysis as advice creates retainer/consultation-boundary risk.

**Solution:** Brief must show **current self-reportable facts**, **outstanding gaps still open**, **engagement phase that produced the meeting**, and **secondary signals as agenda (not parallel outreach)** — framed as prep for consultation, not as a concluded service opinion — so that the consultant walks in current and the meeting remains the professional advice moment.

**Handoff:** pm (Live brief content contract); sync seat 1 on what Analysis may assert vs desk-safe language
