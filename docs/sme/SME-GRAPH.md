## 0. How this graph was built

Read Seed (bets, §8 trust/consent, §13 known unknowns, §14 never-invent, §15 chair hints, doctrine), World (value chain, hard gates, open-box, engagement laws), Personas (consultant desk vs house-global / per-tenancy surfaces), Outcomes (consultant hand-over·authorize·receive; engagement machinery as leaf requirements; operator authorship), and How trees (especially `consultant-core`, `operator-configuration-libraries`, `contact-consent`, `operator-activation`, plus acquisition / commercial / book-readiness / reference-data / contact-refresh). Secondary filter: Om Coda failure-mode check (who does the work, who regulates, who gets sued) to sharpen “failure if absent.”

**Residual test:** mint a seat only where the product already committed to a way of working that needs professional knowledge of *how that practice is done well*, and that knowledge is not Founder dump, PM assembly/translation, or CTO wiring.

**Stop rule:** no padding; merge shared failure modes; do not mint Engine 2 / Audit-scope / Hub-rehome / OTP / platform-observability as SME. Seed §15 lanes were pressure-tested, not rubber-stamped.
