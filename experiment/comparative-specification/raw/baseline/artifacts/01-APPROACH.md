# Approach (Contestant 1 — Baseline)

No extra methodology was assigned. Work as a general-purpose agent:

1. Confirm SHA.
2. Map the repository’s product-definition layers (Seed/World/Story/Outcomes/How, Phase Two face, SQL, product face code).
3. Treat later founder-locked wording as law when it explicitly supersedes earlier restatements.
4. Treat as-built code and migrations as evidence of intended machinery **only when** they implement a named law. Do not promote prototype leftovers or Hub parking into product identity.
5. Where layers conflict, record the conflict; do not invent a founder decision.
6. Produce a coherent intended-product specification plus an explicit uncertainty register.
