## Sequence

**A. Substrate.** Session firm is explicit. `firm_id` scopes rows. No demo-tenant default. One walk (`workflowWalk.ts`), one cascade, one worker. Missing store = SA ascribe, not a client catalog.

**B. Honest Ant shell.** Lock plate chrome. Seats are Consultant · Operator · Contact — not Firm, not Register. Smoke here. If the glass lies, later proofs are worthless.

**C. Real book.** Authorize and/or import into `contact_records`. Consultant panes key by those ids. No baked roster.

**D. Audit = reachability.** Verdicts reachable / partial / unreachable. Sequence-ready is this gate. Not a sales score, not pathway scoring, not a minimum-count ceremony.

**E. Send gates before any campaign.** Consent / halt / silence suppress enroll+send. Opt-out is an event. Live outbound only through dispatch. Until E holds, Agents must not fire.

**F. Automations as the operations brain.** Smallest legal workflow: trigger · rule · action; canvas may chain. Named listen catalog at Global or Local grain (this form vs all of that kind). Not “any row changed.” This is the machine the rest hangs on.

**G. Handle after the node.** Next step picks from every write that run made (`previousWrites` / `chainWrites`). If/Switch and mapped boxes resolve against that run — not a client field catalog, not “latest audit for this firm.” Completions are events; do not mint an events table that copies the write.

**H. Two action kinds, kept apart.** In-app: tag, audit, who-this-applies-to, form. Agents: assign existing or from template. Forks are not actions. Full operations/exit catalogs stay open (do not invent them to look done).

**I. Agents authorship to 15:43.** Sequence is a blank canvas of campaigns. Operator sets order deployed, per firm or default, plus overrides (nested like CSS). Objective is first on a campaign. Two objectives → two campaigns. No qualify node. No Automations triggers on this canvas.

**J. Override this / Override content.** Override this reveals channels as wires. Override content expands the composer inside the channel. Attempt rules ≠ channel rules; attempts are not at the channel. Opt-in / re-engagement / reactivation are sequence templates, not objective names.

**K. Goal met is the endpoint.** Once met, met. Escalation: other agent, other workflow, or end. Housing is Local event + subscription (AD-11), not a goal table or destination column. Story §7 wording promotion stays open.

**L. Dual enroll, one actuation.** Automations enrolls on an event. Agents starts a mission now (Add contact; Every firm | Select from). Both land in cascade/worker. One channel owns the attempt; forward-only when criteria are met; live inbound may override an in-flight attempt.

**M. Bind.** House authors libraries. Tenancy binds packs under the firm identity. Consultant inhabits; does not author. No templates → no templates (empty real). Without bind, this is a single-desk toy, not an agency product.

**N. Engine 2.** One client, one motion. Reactivation first if worthy; else one nudge consolidating self-reportable needs. Other signals ride the meeting brief — never parallel outreach.

**O. Record.** Activity is a tree of outreach. Do not attach it left to the trigger that created the agent. Armed / Active / Silenced readable as words, not color-only. Lineage is queryable separately; do not mint a lineage module from the sentence.

**P. Meeting booked is the loop win.** Path from this firm’s book through motion and gates to `meeting_booked` is visible when rows exist. Live brief hydrates from facts. **Kernel success state is this trace, on Ant, with SM-2’s authored pair bound and both enroll doors live, and SM-3’s halt honored.**

**Q. Eligibility wrap.** Versioned IRCC-shaped reference as data. Open-box rules, togglable families, inspectable outcomes. Re-evaluate as facts and reference move. Pathway pass = any assertion, not one favoured pathway. Without Q, P is consented sequencing, not “don’t recheck every file.”

**R. Fact classes and forms.** Self-reportable may occupy a form; document-dependent never become questions; employer-directed asks go to the firm. Data-request form **is** this run’s required-data listing. Forms live in Automations, not Agents. Form-as-own-node vs embedded stays unlocked (lean embedded). Remaining output classes stay open.

**S. Test, Run, Wait.** Test data on the trigger. Run now vs arm. Wait is an additive graph kind (approval Consultant|Operator, or duration) — resume this run. Walker Wait vs `024_wait_occupancy.sql` alignment is an SA/engineer cut, not filled here.

**T. Contact seat.** Firm-branded touchpoints (consent, form, booking). Workspace until opt-out. Silence is a state the consultant can see. Never-opted-in is also silenced.

**U. Operator recover.** Cross-firm health, tenancy drill-down, support context, audit of house actions, halt. A failing tenancy must be recoverable without the consultant inventing ops.

**V. Known-user session.** OTP / access-link as specified. `VITE_AUTH_DISABLED` is a local skip, not product law. Multi-consultant roles inside a firm stay open.
