# MASTERY — ready for this test

A cycle is learned when the JOURNEY **exit capability** is true **and** you can do the proofs without the chapter open.

---

## Four targets

1. **Construction.** The program runs and does what you intended.
2. **Syntax.** You can produce the required C# from a blank file.
3. **Exam language.** You can say the slide’s word **and** retrieve the slide’s tables.
4. **Foundation.** Every meaningful token you typed this cycle has a meaning you can say. Not mixed-exam content. **Magic is a fail** even if the exam drill is green.

Any three without the fourth (when foundation was due) is not ready.

---

## Proofs (every cycle)

| Proof | Demand |
|---|---|
| **Predict** | Before Run: what will appear, fail, or stay the same? |
| **Explain** | What was constructed? Slide’s **EXAM** name? **FOUNDATION** meaning of the tokens? |
| **Break** | Remove or misspell. Syntax error or logic error (PDF’s two kinds)? |
| **Reconstruct** | A second `.cs` file, chapter closed — no token unexplained. |
| **Exam** | Cycle drill, answers not in view. Do not drill foundation as if it were the sitting. |
